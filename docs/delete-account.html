Delete Your Account

You can delete your Score Day account directly from within the app at any time. Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to delete your account

1
Open the Score Day app on your device.
2
Tap the Profile icon at the bottom of the screen.
3
Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
4
Confirm by tapping Yes, delete everything in the warning dialog.

Warning: Deleting your account will permanently remove your profile, all tasks, scores, streaks, challenges, and any other data associated with your account. This action cannot be reversed.
